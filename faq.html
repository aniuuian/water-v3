Water quality
Every unit is matched to local water conditions with multi-stage purification (sediment, carbon, RO/UV where applicable). Many models show a TDS reading so you can see water quality at a glance.
TDS (total dissolved solids) is a measure of what is in your water. Our purifiers are tuned to bring it into a clean, safe range for everyday drinking.
