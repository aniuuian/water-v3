Frequently asked

How fast will I get a reply?
WhatsApp messages are usually picked up in under two minutes during business hours. Email enquiries receive a response within one business day.
Can I speak to someone in my local language?
Yes. Our support team covers English, Igbo, Yoruba, Hausa and Mandarin Chinese. Pick your preferred language and the conversation will follow.
Do you offer on-site visits?
For larger orders or business customers we arrange scheduled visits inside our service cities. Book through the form and the team will propose a window.
Where do I send press or partnership enquiries?
Use the form and pick “Press / Partnerships” as the topic. The comms team triages these within 48 hours and will reply with next steps.
How do I contact the Data Protection Officer?
Pick “Data privacy (DPO)” in the form. You can also email dpo@aqualease.ng for any NDPR-related request — access, deletion or correction.
